# Plant AI – Advertising Storyboards

Production-ready storyboards for the three advertising concepts in `AD_CONCEPTS.md`.

## Video 1 — “3 Seconds Challenge”

**Duration:** ~18–20 seconds  
**Format:** 9:16

| Time | Visual / Camera | On-screen text / Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Tight plant close-up. Slow push-in. | **“Can you identify this plant?”** / light suspense music |
| 2–3s | Same plant, countdown appears. | **“3”** / tick |
| 3–4s | Same shot. | **“2”** / tick |
| 4–5s | Same shot. | **“1”** / tick |
| 5–6s | Quick transition toward the phone. | **“Let’s ask AI.”** / short whoosh |
| 6–10s | Real Plant AI recording: Home → Analysis. | App's **“Analyzing your plant…”** / subtle electronic pulse |
| 10–13s | Real Result screen. | **“AI got it.”** / success ding |
| 13–16s | Result/plant visual. | **“Could you identify it in 3 seconds?”** |
| 16–20s | Clean Plant AI end card. | **“Plant AI”** / **“Try it yourself.”** |

**Editing note:** Remove unnecessary empty transition shots. Keep the countdown, app analysis, and result reveal as the main rhythm of the ad.

## Video 2 — “Know Before You Touch”

**Duration:** ~22–24 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9

| Time | Visual / Camera | On-screen text / Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | POV forest walk; trail, foliage and natural movement. | Natural forest ambience + subtle music |
| 4–7s | Camera slows on an unfamiliar wild plant beside the trail. | **“What is this plant?”** |
| 7–10s | Close-up of a hand approaching the plant and stopping before contact. | **“Let’s learn before we touch it.”** / light suspense |
| 10–13s | Real Plant AI recording: photo capture → Analysis. | **“Analyzing your plant…”** / camera shutter + soft processing sound |
| 13–16s | Real Result screen with plant identity. | **“Now you know what it is.”** / ding |
| 16–19s | Rear/POV view of the hiker putting the phone away and continuing along the trail. | Music relaxes; footsteps return |
| 19–22s | Plant/nature closing shot or clean background. | **“Know before you touch.”** |
| 22–24s | Plant AI logo and CTA. | **“Try Plant AI.”** |

**Editing note:** The ad should communicate identification, not a toxicity verdict. Avoid copy such as “safe”, “not poisonous”, or “toxic” unless the product is later extended with a reliable safety-information source.

## Video 3 — “Plant Parent”

**Duration:** ~20–23 seconds  
**Format:** 9:16  
**Status:** Detailed storyboard / concept

| Time | Visual / Camera | On-screen text / Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Parent and child walking through a sunny park or garden. Child points at a plant. | Child: **“What is this?”** / warm ambient sound |
| 4–7s | They take a few steps and the child points at another plant. | Child: **“What about this one?”** |
| 7–10s | Fast cuts of 2–3 plants and the child's growing curiosity. | Child: **“And this? And that?”** / playful rhythm |
| 10–13s | Parent smiles, gives the phone to the child. | Parent: **“Go discover it yourself.”** |
| 13–17s | Real Plant AI recording: child captures a plant → Analysis → result. | **“Analyzing your plant…”** / gentle processing sound |
| 17–20s | Child excitedly shows the result to the parent. | Success ding + warm music |
| 20–23s | Clean family/nature ending card. | **“Turn curiosity into discovery.”** / **Plant AI** |

**Creative note:** The emotional payoff is not simply identifying a plant; it is turning a repeated “What is that?” moment into a small discovery experience shared between parent and child.

## Production Standards

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical for social-first concepts; 16:9 horizontal for the YouTube concept.
- **Recommended resolution:** 1080×1920 for vertical videos; 1920×1080 for horizontal videos.
- **Product UI:** Use real Plant AI app recordings for analysis and result scenes.
- **Accessibility:** Add readable subtitles/captions for dialogue and key messages.
- **Sound design:** Use music and SFX to support curiosity, analysis, result, and CTA moments.
- **Visual consistency:** Keep typography, colors, logo treatment, and overall visual language consistent with the Plant AI app.
- **Final frame:** Keep the closing frame simple with the Plant AI logo, product name, and one clear CTA.
