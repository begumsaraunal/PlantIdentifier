# Plant AI – Advertising Video Concepts

Three advertising concepts developed for Part 2 of the Scate AI case study. Each concept targets a different user motivation while keeping the product promise consistent: identify plants from a photo.

## 1. “3 Seconds Challenge”

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical / short-form social
- **Duration:** ~18–20 seconds
- **Angle:** Entertainment, AI capability, speed
- **Target:** General social media users
- **Hook:** **“Can you identify this plant?”**
- **Core message:** **“You had 3 seconds. AI didn't need them.”**

### Concept Flow

- **0–2 sec:** Close-up of an unfamiliar plant. Question appears: **“Can you identify this plant?”**
- **2–5 sec:** Three-second countdown: **3 → 2 → 1**.
- **5–6 sec:** **“Let’s ask AI.”**
- **6–10 sec:** Real Plant AI app recording: image analysis.
- **10–13 sec:** Real result screen reveals the identified plant.
- **13–16 sec:** Challenge payoff: **“Could you identify it in 3 seconds?”**
- **16–20 sec:** Plant AI logo and CTA: **“Try it yourself.”**

### Production Direction

The first version was tightened during editing by removing empty transition shots and making the countdown the central visual device. Real app footage is used for the analysis and result rather than generating a fictional interface.

## 2. “Know Before You Touch”

- **Format:** 16:9 horizontal / YouTube
- **Duration:** ~22–24 seconds
- **Angle:** Curiosity, exploration, practical discovery
- **Target:** Outdoor and nature enthusiasts
- **Hook:** **“What is this plant?”**
- **Key line:** **“Let’s learn before we touch it.”**

### Concept Flow

- **0–4 sec:** POV forest walk with natural outdoor atmosphere.
- **4–7 sec:** An unfamiliar wild plant is noticed beside the trail.
- **7–10 sec:** A hand approaches the plant but stops before touching it.
- **10–13 sec:** The user takes a photo and opens Plant AI. Real app recording: **“Analyzing your plant…”**
- **13–16 sec:** Real result screen reveals the plant identity.
- **16–19 sec:** The hiker continues along the trail.
- **19–22 sec:** Closing message: **“Know before you touch.”**
- **22–24 sec:** Plant AI logo and CTA: **“Try Plant AI.”**

### Production Direction

The concept creates a small moment of uncertainty in nature and resolves it through identification. The advertisement does **not** claim that Plant AI determines whether a plant is toxic or safe; the app is shown doing what it actually does: identifying the plant.

## 3. “Plant Parent”

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical / family lifestyle
- **Duration:** ~20–23 seconds
- **Angle:** Curiosity, learning, shared discovery
- **Target:** Families, children, and nature-curious users
- **Hook:** A child's repeated questions about plants
- **Key line:** **“Turn curiosity into discovery.”**

### Concept Flow

- **0–4 sec:** A parent and child walk through a park or garden. The child points at a plant: **“What is this?”**
- **4–7 sec:** They encounter another plant. The child asks: **“What about this one?”**
- **7–10 sec:** Fast cuts of more plants. The child continues: **“And this? And that?”**
- **10–13 sec:** The parent smiles and hands the phone to the child: **“Go discover it yourself.”**
- **13–17 sec:** The child scans a plant using the real Plant AI app. **“Analyzing your plant…” → result**
- **17–20 sec:** The child excitedly shows the result to the parent. A short confirmation sound and warm music.
- **20–23 sec:** Closing card: **“Turn curiosity into discovery.”** / **Plant AI**

### Production Direction

This concept positions Plant AI as a lightweight discovery tool that helps turn children's natural curiosity into an interactive experience. The story intentionally focuses on exploration rather than presenting the app as a replacement for learning or expert knowledge.

---

## Shared Production Principles

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical for social-first concepts; 16:9 horizontal for the YouTube concept.
- **Recommended resolution:** 1080 × 1920 for vertical videos; 1920 × 1080 for horizontal videos.
- **Real product footage:** App screens should be captured from the actual Plant AI build.
- **Silent viewing:** Key messages should remain understandable through on-screen text/subtitles.
- **No unsupported claims:** The advertising should not imply guaranteed identification or toxicity/safety analysis.
- **Final frame:** Plant AI logo, app name, and one clear CTA.
